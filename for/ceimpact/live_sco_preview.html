1. Federal NCVIA VIS/VAERS Mandates & State Standing-Order Scope of Immunization Practice

Under the National Childhood Vaccine Injury Act (42 U.S.C. 300aa-26), immunizing pharmacists and qualified pharmacy technicians must furnish the current CDC Vaccine Information Statement prior to every dose of any Vaccine Injury Table antigen (regardless of recipient age) and permanently log the VIS edition date, date provided, manufacturer, lot number, and administering provider's name and title. Clinically significant adverse events listed on the VAERS Reportable Events Table must be filed with VAERS irrespective of whether the pharmacist believes the vaccine caused the reaction.

2. SIRVA-Safe Deltoid Needle Sizing & Immediate Epinephrine Management of Vaccine Anaphylaxis

To prevent Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration (SIRVA) into the subacromial/subdeltoid bursa, CDC ACIP guidelines require a 90-degree intramuscular injection into the central deltoid 2 to 3 fingerbreadths below the acromion process, using a 1-inch needle for adults <=152 lb (70 kg), 1 to 1.5 inches for women 152-200 lb and men 152-260 lb, and a 1.5-inch needle for women >200 lb (90 kg) or men >260 lb (118 kg). Acute vaccine-associated anaphylaxis requires immediate intramuscular 1 mg/mL (1:1,000) epinephrine (0.01 mg/kg; adult 0.3 to 0.5 mg) into the mid-outer thigh (vastus lateralis), repeated every 5 to 15 minutes as needed with prompt 911 EMS activation.
