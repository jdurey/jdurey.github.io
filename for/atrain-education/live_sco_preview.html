Part 1 · Source Regulatory / Clinical Digest

1. Structured Suicide Risk Screening (C-SSRS) & Imminent Harm Warm Handoff

Under ANCC and state-mandated suicide prevention protocols, a positive screen accompanied by an active plan, intent, and lethal means access constitutes imminent risk. Clinicians must maintain continuous visual or live-line observation, initiate collaborative lethal-means safety planning, and execute a warm handoff to emergency behavioral health crisis services rather than relying solely on a passive written crisis card.

2. Unintentional Acetaminophen Supratherapeutic Dosing & N-Acetylcysteine (NAC) Timing

FDA labeling caps prescription combination opioid-acetaminophen tablets at 325 mg acetaminophen per dosage unit and recommends a maximum adult daily intake of 4,000 mg (or lower in chronic hepatic impairment). Because multi-symptom OTC cold remedies silently stack acetaminophen, nurses must reconcile total 24-hour gram intake across all OTC and PRN sources and apply the Rumack-Matthew nomogram only to single acute ingestions with a known timestamp.
