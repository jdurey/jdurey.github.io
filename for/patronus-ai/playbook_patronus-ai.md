# Patronus AI Week-1 Rollout Plan for Applied AI Workflow & Eval Gate Lead

## Objective
Enable Patronus AI teams and enterprise customers to adopt the multi-model rubric calibration and domain rubric decomposition kit with integrated human escalation checkpoints, ensuring smooth customer AI workflow enablement and verdict integrity.

## SOP Steps
1. **Kickoff Workshop**: Conduct a 2-hour hands-on session with domain experts and customer success teams to review the normalized SOP decomposition templates and rubric calibration methodology.
2. **Deploy Workflow DAG**: Activate the 4-stage pipeline in Patronus AI’s evaluation platform, ensuring all multi-model judge-trust ensembles and policy gates are live.
3. **Configure Human Checkpoints**: Define explicit escalation triggers in the workflow for ambiguous or policy-flagged cases, assigning human reviewers with clear audit responsibilities.
4. **Run Pilot Sessions**: Execute 100+ evaluation sessions using real customer domain inputs, monitoring false-pass rates and escalation volumes.
5. **Feedback Loop Integration**: Collect human reviewer corrections and update rubric calibrations weekly to reduce escalation load and improve automation confidence.

## Human Checkpoint Rules
- Escalate all inputs with missing or ambiguous schema fields.
- Escalate any detected PII exposure or unauthorized checkout attempts.
- Escalate hallucinated or unsupported domain premises.
- Require human signoff on all policy-flagged cases before final verdict release.

## Adoption Metrics
- Target 0% false-pass rate within first 2 weeks.
- Reduce human escalations by 30% after 4 weeks through rubric refinement.
- Achieve >90% human reviewer agreement on rubric scores.
- Track and resolve all detected reward-gaming or prompt-injection attempts within 24 hours.

## Continuous Improvement
- Weekly calibration sync meetings with domain experts.
- Monthly update of SOP decomposition templates based on new enterprise workflows.
- Quarterly audit of policy gate effectiveness and human checkpoint compliance.

This plan ensures Patronus AI’s enterprise customers gain immediate value from the Day-1 deliverable while building trust and adoption through transparent, auditable AI judgment workflows.
