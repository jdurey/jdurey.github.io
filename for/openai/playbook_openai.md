# OpenAI Academy Enablement Workflow: Week-1 Rollout Plan

## Objective
Launch a robust, role-based AI enablement workflow for non-technical OpenAI teams, ensuring safe, relevant, and policy-aligned onboarding through hands-on labs and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.

## Day 1-2: Setup & Stakeholder Alignment
- Brief Academy leadership and department heads (Trust & Safety, Customer Success, Developer Relations) on workflow objectives and escalation protocols.
- Configure the intake form and scenario router with real OpenAI role/dept mappings and product-specific scenario templates.
- Assign at least two human facilitators per department for checkpoint reviews and escalation handling.

## Day 3-4: Pilot & Human Checkpoint Calibration
- Run a closed pilot with 10-15 non-technical staff from at least three departments.
- Facilitators review all routed scenarios using the provided rubric:
  - Is the scenario relevant to the learner’s OpenAI role and department?
  - Are all outputs free from policy violations (PII, prompt injection, unauthorized data access)?
  - Was escalation triggered appropriately for ambiguous or policy-adjacent cases?
- Log all escalations and review outcomes in the Academy Analytics Dashboard.

## Day 5: Feedback & Iteration
- Hold a daily standup with facilitators to discuss edge cases, escalation trends, and rubric gaps.
- Refine scenario templates and escalation triggers based on pilot feedback.
- Update documentation and SOPs for common issues (e.g. ambiguous roles, PII hints, prompt injection attempts).

## Adoption Metrics
- Track scenario completion rates, escalation frequency, and facilitator review times.
- Set a Week-1 target: 90% of routed scenarios are role-aligned and policy-compliant on first pass, with <10% requiring escalation.
- Collect qualitative feedback from learners and facilitators for continuous improvement.

## Ongoing
- Expand rollout to additional teams, integrating new OpenAI product scenarios and updating rubrics as policies evolve.
- Schedule bi-weekly reviews to ensure alignment with OpenAI’s latest product and policy updates.
