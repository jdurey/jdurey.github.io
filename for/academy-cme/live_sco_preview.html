1. Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) Sequencing & Monitoring in HFrEF

In hospitalized patients stabilized after acute decompensated heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (LVEF <= 40%), interprofessional care teams (cardiology, inpatient nursing, clinical pharmacy) coordinate four-pillar generic GDMT initiation (angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitor or ACEi/ARB, evidence-based beta-blocker, mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist, and SGLT2 inhibitor) with structured 7-to-14-day renal/potassium follow-up.

2. ACCME Content Validity & Non-Promotional Assessment Standards
