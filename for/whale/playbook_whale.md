# Whale Week-1 Rollout Plan for Principal AI Learning Systems & Assessment Verification Architect

## Objective
Deploy the Whale Zero-Hallucination SOP-to-SCORM 1.2 + QTI Compiler with 4-Vendor Blind-Solver Verification to ensure auditable, zero-drift SOP mastery proof and compliance-ready training packages.

## Step 1: Setup and Integration
- Import Whale SOP playbooks into the Human Creative SOP Parser.
- Validate all SOP inputs against Whale's strict schema, ensuring no missing or ambiguous fields.
- Configure the 4-vendor blind-solver panel with Whale's assessment items for cross-family verification.

## Step 2: Training and Human Checkpoints
- Train L&D and compliance teams on interpreting JSON contract outputs, focusing on flags for schema validity, policy risk, and escalation triggers.
- Establish human checkpoint rules: escalate any flagged missing fields, PII exposure, policy traps, or hallucinated content.
- Use the Whale AI Workflow Router to enforce structured JSON governance and audit trails.

## Step 3: Pilot Execution
- Run pilot SOP-to-SCORM compilations on key Whale workflows (e.g., warehouse inventory, frontline onboarding).
- Review flagged escalations with compliance officers and refine SOP content or rubric criteria as needed.
- Collect adoption metrics: number of SOPs processed, escalation rates, false-pass reduction, and audit readiness scores.

## Step 4: Continuous Improvement
- Monitor 4-vendor blind-solver results for emerging reward-gaming or policy drift patterns.
- Update SOP schema and rubric guardrails quarterly based on audit feedback.
- Expand training scenarios with distractor diagnostics and workplace scenario checks.

## Adoption Metrics
- Target 0% false-pass rate in blind-solver verification.
- Achieve >95% schema_valid outputs without escalation after pilot refinement.
- Reduce human escalations by 30% through improved SOP normalization.
- Ensure 100% audit compliance with downloadable SCORM 1.2 packages.

This rollout plan ensures Whale’s SOP training workflows are fully compliant, auditable, and scalable with zero hallucination and multi-vendor verified mastery proof.
