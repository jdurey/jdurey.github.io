1. Structural Design-Check Governance & NSPE Ethical Duties in the Minnesota I-35W Gusset-Plate Collapse

NTSB investigation HAR-08/03 determined that the 1967 deck-truss design specified 0.5-inch gusset plates at Node U10 instead of the 1.0-inch thickness required for design shear and compression forces, compounded by 2-inch concrete deck overlays added in 1977 and 1998 plus concentrated construction staging loads in August 2007. Under NSPE Code of Ethics Section II.1.a-b and state P.E. board rules, licensed Professional Engineers must hold paramount public safety, perform independent connection load-rating verification before sealing dead-load increases, and notify proper authorities in writing if engineering judgment is overruled.

2. 2020 NFPA 70 (NEC) Part 1 Revisions: Expanded Dwelling GFCI Scope (210.8) & Exterior Disconnects (230.67 / 230.85)

The 2020 National Electrical Code removed the legacy 125V ceiling in 210.8(A), extending Class A Ground-Fault Circuit-Interrupter protection to all 125V through 250V receptacles installed in listed dwelling zones (including 240V electric clothes dryers in laundry areas and 240V sump or well pumps in basements). Concurrently, new Articles 230.67 and 230.85 require a Type 1 or Type 2 Surge-Protective Device (SPD) on all dwelling services and a readily accessible outdoor emergency disconnect permanently marked for first-responder de-energization.
