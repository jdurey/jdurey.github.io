1. TEAM-CBT Five Secrets of Effective Communication & the Disarming Technique

Before introducing cognitive restructuring or behavioral exposure methods, TEAM-CBT prioritizes accurate empathy using the Five Secrets of Effective Communication. In the Disarming Technique, the clinician locates verifiable truth inside a frustrated or critical client statement (even when distorted) rather than defending clinical expertise or prematurely debating cognitive distortions.

2. Paradoxical Agenda Setting (Assessment of Resistance) Prior to Methods

In TEAM-CBT, jumping straight into symptom-reduction techniques when outcome or process resistance is present triggers 'yes-but' stalemate loops. Clinicians use Magic Button, Positive Reframing, and Externalization of Resistance so the client voices their own values-driven reasons for change before selecting cognitive or exposure methods.
