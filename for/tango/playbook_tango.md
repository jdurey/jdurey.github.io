## Tango Week-1 Rollout Plan for SOP-to-SCORM 1.2 Compiler & 4-Vendor Verification Kit

### Objective
Deploy and operationalize the zero-hallucination SOP-to-SCORM 1.2 compilation and 4-vendor blind-solver verification pipeline to ensure enterprise LMS compliance and audit readiness.

### Step 1: Environment Setup
- Integrate Tango workflow capture API with Human Creative SOP Parser.
- Deploy SOP-to-LMS Compiler Engine on staging environment.
- Configure Judge-Trust 4-vendor panel and Verdict-Integrity repeaters.

### Step 2: Training & Onboarding
- Conduct hands-on training sessions for Tango enablement and L&D teams on SOP normalization and SCORM packaging.
- Review human checkpoint criteria and escalation protocols.
- Distribute adoption rubrics focusing on zero hallucination and policy compliance.

### Step 3: Pilot Execution
- Select 3 live Tango workflows with embedded assessments.
- Run full pipeline: normalization, compilation, 4-vendor verification, and policy checkpointing.
- Document detected risks, escalations, and remediation steps.

### Step 4: Human Checkpoint Protocols
- Establish a dedicated review team for flagged ambiguous or policy-risk SOP items.
- Use structured JSON audit logs to triage escalations.
- Provide feedback loops to update SOP templates and scoring rubrics.

### Step 5: Metrics & Continuous Improvement
- Track adoption metrics: number of workflows processed, escalations, false-pass rates.
- Monitor 4-vendor panel consensus scores and verdict-integrity repeatability.
- Iterate on rubric calibration and SOP normalization rules based on feedback.

### Step 6: Full Production Rollout
- Gradually onboard all Tango enablement workflows.
- Automate human checkpoint alerts and reporting dashboards.
- Schedule biweekly reviews to refine policy flags and escalation thresholds.

This plan ensures Tango’s SOP-to-SCORM pipeline delivers deterministic, audit-ready training content with zero hallucination risk and enterprise-grade compliance verification from Day 1.
