Part 1 · Source Regulatory / Clinical Digest

1. Modified Barium Swallow (MBS) Physiological Impairment vs. Bolus Clearance

Videofluoroscopic swallowing evaluation identifies biomechanical pathophysiology across oral, pharyngeal, and esophageal phases rather than functioning as a pass/fail diet test. Clinicians distinguish penetration from silent aspiration (PAS Score 8, bolus entering below the vocal folds with zero cough response) and link residue patterns (valleculae vs. pyriform sinuses) to specific hyolaryngeal or pharyngoesophageal segment impairments.

2. Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES / VitalStim) Electrode Placement & Contraindications

Adjunctive dysphagia NMES pairs motor-level sensory-motor recruitment of suprahyoid and infrahyoid musculature with active volitional Effortful or Mendelsohn swallows. Clinicians screen strictly for implanted demand cardiac pacemakers/ICDs, carotid sinus placement, active anterior neck malignancy, or absent volitional swallow initiation before applying anterior cervical current.
