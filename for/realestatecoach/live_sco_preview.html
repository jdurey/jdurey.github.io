Part 1 · Source Regulatory / Playbook Digest

1. Mandatory Written Buyer Agreement Prior to Touring

Before taking a prospective buyer on an in-person or live virtual property tour, agents must execute a written buyer-representation or touring agreement that states the specific, objectively ascertainable compensation amount or rate, prohibits open-ended fee ranges, and discloses that broker commissions are not set by law and are fully negotiable.

2. Fair Housing Guardrails During Neighborhood Coaching

When prospects ask whether an area is 'family-friendly' or 'safe', compliant agents avoid demographic characterizations or subjective safety steering and instead point clients to objective municipal, transit, and third-party public data sources.
