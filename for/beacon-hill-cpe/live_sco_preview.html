1. Form 1040 Material Participation & IRC Sec. 469 Rental Real Estate Exceptions

Under IRC Sec. 469, passive activity losses generally offset only passive income unless the taxpayer satisfies one of the seven Temp. Reg. 1.469-5T material participation tests or qualifies under the $25,000 active-participation rental allowance (phasing out at $100,000 to $150,000 MAGI) or Sec. 469(c)(7) real estate professional rules (>750 hours and >50% of personal services).

2. Private Equity European vs. American Deal-by-Deal Distribution Waterfalls

In a whole-fund (European) waterfall, Limited Partners receive 100% of contributed capital across all realized and unrealized deals plus the preferred return hurdle (typically 8% compounded) before the General Partner collects catch-up and 20% carried interest. American deal-by-deal waterfalls accelerate carry on early winners, requiring GP clawback escrows to protect LPs against subsequent write-downs.
