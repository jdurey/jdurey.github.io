# Writer AI Adoption Lab: Week-1 Rollout Playbook

## Objective
Enable non-technical enterprise teams to adopt Writer’s AI suite safely and effectively, using structured workflows, hands-on labs, and robust escalation for policy boundaries.

## Day 1-2: Intake & Mapping
- Collect real intake requests from Marketing, Support, Legal, and HR teams.
- Use the Enablement Copilot to parse requests and map roles to Writer features (Templates, Content Guardrails, Knowledge Graph, PII Redaction).
- Log all ambiguous or policy-sensitive requests for review.

## Day 3: Policy Routing & Escalation
- Route requests through Writer’s policy matrix: check for PII, brand compliance, legal review, and admin overrides.
- For any flagged request (PII, data export, ambiguous mapping), require human checkpoint by the enablement lead or designated SME.
- Document escalation reasons and outcomes in the adoption tracker.

## Day 4: Hands-On Training Labs
- Generate tailored training scenarios for each team using real customer data and Writer features.
- Facilitate live lab sessions, ensuring each participant completes a workflow aligned with their role and policy requirements.
- Use adoption rubrics to assess completion and identify friction points.

## Day 5: Metrics & Feedback
- Collect usage data: number of requests processed, escalations, training completions, and user feedback.
- Analyze adoption metrics (e.g., % of users completing labs, average time to resolution, escalation rate).
- Update enablement content and SOPs based on real-world friction and feedback.

## Human Checkpoint Rules
- All requests involving PII, data export, or ambiguous mapping must be escalated.
- Only designated enablement leads or SMEs may approve policy-boundary workflows.
- Maintain an audit log of all escalations and resolutions.

## Adoption Metrics
- Track: workflow completion rate, escalation frequency, user satisfaction (post-lab survey), and time-to-adoption per team.
- Use metrics to prioritize follow-up training and SOP updates.
