Part 1 · Source Regulatory / Clinical Digest

1. ADA First-Line Non-Opioid Multimodal Analgesia & PDMP Verification

Clinical guidelines from the American Dental Association recommend synergistic NSAID plus acetaminophen therapy (e.g., ibuprofen 400 mg + acetaminophen 500 mg) as first-line management for acute nociceptive dental pain following surgical extractions in patients without contraindications. When narrow clinical exceptions warrant a short-course opioid, DEA MATE Act and state rules require Prescription Drug Monitoring Program (PDMP) review, lowest effective immediate-release dosing, and co-prescribing counseling.

2. CDC Dental Unit Waterline (DUWL) EPA Potable Standard (<= 500 CFU/mL)

CDC Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings require coolant/irrigant water delivered by handpieces and ultrasonic scalers during nonsurgical procedures to meet EPA regulatory standards for drinking water (<= 500 CFU/mL of heterotrophic water bacteria). Surgical procedures involving bone cutting or exposure of vascularized tissue require sterile saline or sterile water delivered through sterile single-use or autoclavable bulb/tubing systems.
